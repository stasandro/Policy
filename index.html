Future Historians

A YouTube documentary series made as if by historians in the year 2126, trying to make sense of everyday life in the 2020s from the few fragments that survived: objects, scraps of text, and our landfills.

Every film is satire. The Institute, its scholars and every event after 2026 are fictional. Illustrations are original and made for the series; narration is performed by synthetic voices that do not imitate real people.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@futurehistorians
